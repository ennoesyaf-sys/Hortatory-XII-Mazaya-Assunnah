Teks dan struktur · 10 menit

What is a hortatory exposition?

Sumber: Step Up 3 hlm. 94.

Teks yang menyajikan argumen untuk mendukung opini penulis, lalu mengusulkan saran di akhir. Contoh: editorial, letter to the editor, letter to politician.

ThesisSudut pandang penulis, pratinjau argumen, bisa berupa pertanyaan atau pernyataan emosional.
ArgumentsRangkaian pendukung thesis; tiap argumen di paragraf baru.
RecommendationSaran tentang apa yang sebaiknya atau sebaiknya tidak dilakukan.

Language features 1/5 · 5 menit

Modal verbs and modal adverbs

Sumber: Step Up 3 hlm. 95 (modal verbs, gambar 4.8), hlm. 96 (contoh dari teks), hlm. 98-99 (modal adverbs).

Modal verbs menyatakan kemungkinan, keharusan, kemampuan, atau saran: can could may might shall should must will would need to. Modal adverbs (certainly, probably, absolutely) menambah makna modal verb.

Klik semua modal verb dan modal adverb.

Students [could] work on getting enough sleep, and rain [should] not be an excuse if the student is truly committed.

You [should] [absolutely] take up that course.

Language features 2/5 · 5 menit

Emotive words

Sumber: Step Up 3 hlm. 95 (definisi), hlm. 87 dan 93 (kalimat dari teks).

Kata pilihan yang menunjukkan perasaan untuk memengaruhi pembaca agar setuju. Contoh dari buku: betrayed beautifully love justice wonderful.

Klik kata bernada emosi.

It’s [unfair] to classmates who come on time and [frustrating] for teachers who have to deal with constant interruptions.

Although technology is a [wonderful] thing, it is also the main cause of our laziness.

[Sadly], going the easy route is not recommended.

Language features 3/5 · 5 menit

Abstract nouns

Sumber: Step Up 3 hlm. 91 (tabel 4.1 dan contoh kalimat), hlm. 95 (definisi), hlm. 96 (contoh dari teks).

Kata benda untuk ide, kualitas, atau keadaan yang tidak bisa dilihat, didengar, disentuh, dicium, atau dicicipi. Contoh: belief culture consideration kindness courage.

Klik semua abstract noun.

Coming late to school has become a common [problem] among students today.

Learning to be punctual helps students develop [responsibility] and [discipline].

[Kindness] is something almost everyone appreciates.

Language features 4/5 · 5 menit

Action verbs

Sumber: Step Up 3 hlm. 95 (definisi), hlm. 96 (contoh dari teks).

Kata kerja yang menggambarkan aksi fisik atau mental. Contoh dari teks: arrive miss affects disrupts stay watching.

Klik semua action verb.

This not only [affects] their own learning but also [disrupts] the class.

Many students [arrive] after classes have started and [miss] out on important lessons.

Language features 5/5 · 5 menit

Connectives

Sumber: Step Up 3 hlm. 95 (definisi), hlm. 96 (contoh dari teks), hlm. 99 (time connectives, tabel 4.3).

Penghubung antar ide: while although however yet if or and. Time connectives (hlm. 99): first then finally eventually afterwards in the end.

Klik semua connective.

[While] helping at home is important, time management is just as essential.

[However], none of them will work unless the student truly wants to change.

[Eventually], I started losing weight and gaining muscle.
